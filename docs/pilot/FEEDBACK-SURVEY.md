# Pilot feedback survey

Please answer honestly. Critical answers help us most.

1. How long did it take from start to the first governed action?
2. What was the hardest part of the setup?
3. How many agent actions were governed? How many were blocked or escalated?
4. Did Aegisora stop anything that should not have run? Describe one example.
5. Did Aegisora stop anything that should have been allowed? Describe one example.
6. How long did an approval take your reviewer on average? Was the approval screen clear?
7. What would your security or compliance team need before approving this for production?
8. Which tools or systems would you want connected next?
9. Who in your company would own and pay for this?
10. Would you continue after the pilot? If yes, what would you consider a fair monthly price? If no, why not?
11. On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend Aegisora to a colleague?
12. Anything else we should know?
